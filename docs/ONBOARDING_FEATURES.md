# Onboarding: key features

The short list to paste into an email when inviting someone to try Remindly,
alongside the link to https://www.remindly.care. Each entry is written to be
copied as it stands: plain language, one or two sentences, no jargon.

**Keep it true.** Only features that are live in production belong here. When
a pull request ships something a new user would notice, update this file in
the same PR — add an entry, or correct one the change made wrong. Anything
here that is not on the site is a promise the product then breaks in the
first email someone reads.

Last checked against the live site: 2026-09-25 (version 0.14.0).

---

## The full list

**Reminder phone calls, not another app.** Remindly rings your parent's own
phone, landline or mobile, at the right time and says the reminder out loud.
They press 1 when it's done or 2 to be reminded again shortly. There's nothing
to install and nothing to read.

**Nobody is called without agreeing first.** The first call only asks
permission, and says who set it up. Pressing 9 on any call stops the calls,
and you can pick the hours calls are allowed, anywhere from 6am to 10pm their
time.

**You hear what happened, without ringing to ask.** For medication, you get an
email when a reminder is marked done, and another if the time passes and
nobody marks it. For reminders where being late matters, you're told as soon
as the first call goes unanswered.

**Or a tablet that speaks.** If there's a tablet or computer in the house,
Remindly can speak from its screen instead, with one big Done button. There's
no password or account for your parent: you bookmark a link on their device,
or read them six numbers over the phone.

**Set up from anywhere.** You create the reminders from your own phone or
computer: morning tablets, a glass of water, Thursday's appointment. They can
repeat daily or weekly, and follow your parent's clock, not yours.

**Share the load with family.** Several caregivers can be linked to one
person, each choosing which reminders they hear about. A shared task list
shows who's picking up the prescription and who's covering the delivery.

**Free while in beta.** No card, no ads and no trial that quietly ends. If
charging is ever needed, you'll be told well in advance.

## The short version

When four is enough, keep these: they answer the questions a new person is
most likely to have — what it does, whether it is safe for their parent,
what they get out of it, and what it costs.

1. Reminder phone calls, not another app
2. Nobody is called without agreeing first
3. You hear what happened, without ringing to ask
4. Free while in beta

## Worth adding to the email

A line asking them to reply with anything that confused them. At this stage
that feedback is worth more than the signup.
